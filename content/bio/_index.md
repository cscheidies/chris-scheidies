Bios are boring. I make stuff. That's it. Usually music, or writing, or a garden — sometimes a business. Like [Mastiff Systems](https://mastiffsystems.com/) or [So Green Farms](https://www.sogreenfarms.com/). Want to make something cool together? Get in touch. You can catch me playing live with [Flannel Channel](https://www.facebook.com/flannelchannelnebraska) (RIP), [The Drunk Pedestrians](https://www.drunkpedestrians.com/), [Flux Radio](https://www.fluxradioband.com/).
